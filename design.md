# ReiseCeylon Design System

## Overview

ReiseCeylon is a Sri Lankan travel platform built around the idea that meaningful
journeys become lasting memories. The visual identity combines cultural depth,
modern travel technology, wildlife, tropical landscapes, and premium hospitality.

The design system should feel:

- Cultural, but not traditional or dated
- Premium, but not exclusive or intimidating
- Modern, but not cold or overly technical
- Tropical, but not overly bright or playful
- Emotional, but still practical for booking and planning
- International, while remaining clearly connected to Sri Lanka

The visual hierarchy is:

1. Destination photography
2. Clear travel information
3. Functional search and booking controls
4. Brand color as direction and emphasis
5. Cultural details as subtle storytelling

## Brand Identity

The ReiseCeylon logo combines:

- **Elephant** — heritage, strength, memory, wildlife, and Sri Lankan identity
- **Palm tree** — tropical nature and island travel
- **Golden sun** — warmth, hospitality, optimism, and premium experiences
- **Winding path** — exploration, personal journeys, and discovery
- **Ocean teal** — the Indian Ocean, rivers, waterfalls, and movement
- **Airplane** — international travel and adventure
- **Letter R** — the primary monogram for ReiseCeylon

The wordmark uses:

- **Reise** in Forest Green
- **Ceylon** in Heritage Gold
- **Where Memories Find Home** as the official tagline

## Typography

### Font Pairing

ReiseCeylon uses two complementary voices:

1. **Cormorant Garamond** for expressive destination headings, cultural content,
   editorial sections, and premium brand moments.
2. **Manrope** for navigation, forms, booking controls, cards, buttons, tables,
   and application interfaces.

Recommended fallback stack:

```css
--font-display: "Cormorant Garamond", Georgia, serif;
--font-interface: "Manrope", Inter, system-ui, sans-serif;
```

## Editorial advertising placements

Advertising on blog and destination pages uses existing `--muted`, `--hairline`
and interface typography tokens, with translated 10px labels and 24px vertical
padding. Reserve the exact creative dimensions before loading. Use a centered
468×60 banner (320×50 on narrow screens), a 160×300 detail-page margin placement
above 1024px, and a centered 300×250 unit between lower sections. Keep hero imagery,
reading widths, discovery filters, maps and enquiry controls intact. Banner
creatives are isolated in their own documents; their colors are provider-owned.
Page-level Adsterra formats are enabled separately and can add provider-controlled
overlays/popunders; their appearance and frequency need provider-side tuning.
